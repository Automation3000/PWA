TABREED ETS LOCATOR - GOOGLE SHEET SETUP
==========================================

Instructions:
1. Create a new Google Sheet.
2. Rename the sheet tab at the bottom to "Data".
3. Copy the columns below and paste them into Row 1 (A1 to F1).

|     A    |   B   |     C    |     D     |      E      |      F      |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
| ETS Name | Plant | Latitude | Longitude |   Comment   | Timestamp   |

Note: 
- Make sure exactly these names are in Row 1.
- You no longer need an "ID" column.
- Timestamp will be generated automatically by the script.
